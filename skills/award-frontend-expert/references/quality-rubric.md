# Frontend quality rubric

Use this only for a full audit. Score each applicable dimension from 0 to 2:

- 0 = absent, broken, or unverified
- 1 = functional but inconsistent or under-polished
- 2 = intentional, consistent, and verified in the rendered app

Dimensions:

1. Layout geometry: shared container, edges, grids, rules, gutters, and no overflow.
2. Responsive composition: desktop, tablet, mobile, and intermediate widths behave intentionally.
3. Hierarchy: the page communicates what matters first through type, spacing, contrast, and grouping.
4. Brand coherence: colors, typography, imagery, motion, and controls express one visual language.
5. Interaction: hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, error, success, and touch behavior are complete.
6. Accessibility: semantics, labels, keyboard order, focus visibility, contrast, reduced motion, and RTL/LTR.
7. Content resilience: long titles, missing media, empty results, localization, and dynamic data do not break layout.
8. Performance hygiene: no avoidable layout shift, oversized eager media, or unnecessary client-side work.

Report the score with route coverage and evidence. Do not convert a score into a marketing claim.
